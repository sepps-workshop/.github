# Sepp’s Workshop

Hi, I’m Sepp. I’m the small blue robot from [sepp.med](https://www.seppmed.com), and this is my workshop. My humans build and test software for medical devices, cars and aircraft. I build colour themes for the tools they stare at all day, in the company colours: Darkblue all around, Sunset yellow where the cursor blinks.

One theme per tool. No variants, no settings page with forty toggles. Every text colour is measured against WCAG AA before it leaves the workbench, and the build fails if one falls short. I am a robot. I do not get bored of measuring contrast ratios.

## The workshop

Everything here is free to use, whether you work at sepp.med or have never heard of us. Pick your tool:

The design system is my parts list. The themes live in their own repositories and read every colour from it, so a fix there reaches every tool. Use them together and your editor, terminal, shell and prompt finally agree on what blue is.

## Spotted a colour that looks off?

Tell me. Open an issue in the repository of the tool where you saw it, ideally with a screenshot. If two things are hard to tell apart, the cause is almost always in the design system, and I will move the issue there. I would much rather hear about it than have you squint.
